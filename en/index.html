  • Fully comprehensive insurance with an excess of 900 € per claim, third-party liability and 24-hour roadside assistance.
  • In case of an accident, tell us and the police straight away and fill in the joint accident report. If anything is stolen, report it and send us the police report within 24 hours.

Countries you can visit
  • Spain and Portugal, and much of Europe too (France, Italy, Germany, the United Kingdom…). If you leave Spain, let us know a week in advance so we can extend the insurance.

Pick-up, return and extensions
  • At pick-up we explain everything calmly and sign the handover report with the vehicle's condition together. When you return it, we check it again.
  • It comes with a full diesel tank and must be returned full. Return is by 7 pm on the agreed day.
  • To extend your rental, let us know 3 days in advance (subject to availability). Returning it early does not entitle you to a refund.
  • If you prefer, we can deliver or collect it somewhere else (airport, hotel…), agreed in advance. This service has an extra charge.
Pets, smoking and proper use
  • No pets allowed.
  • No smoking inside the motorhome.
  • Only the hirer and registered drivers may drive. No alcohol or drugs at the wheel, towing, carrying goods or subletting.

Frequently asked questions

What people ask us most before renting.

Can't find your question? Message us and we will answer you ourselves, usually the same day. Ask us on WhatsApp
How much does it cost to rent a motorhome in Málaga?

With Ruta 340 it costs €120/day in low season, €140/day in mid season (June, September, Easter week, Semana Blanca and the Jerez motorcycle races, according to each year's calendar) and €160/day in high season (July and August), VAT included. Booking 7 days or more in low or mid season, the price drops to €110 and €130/day; in high season, to €150/day from 15 days. Unlimited kilometres from 4 days and fully comprehensive insurance are included.

What is the minimum rental period?

3 days in low season and 7 days in mid and high season. If your trip spans several seasons, the highest minimum applies.

Is there a mileage limit?

Not if you rent for more than 3 days: mileage is unlimited. 3-day rentals include 800 km and each extra kilometre costs €0.28.

Can I drive the motorhome with a car licence?

Yes. The Roller Team Kronos 284 TL weighs 3,500 kg, so a category B licence is enough. The driver must be at least 25 and have held the licence for more than 2 years. If you are not an EU resident, you also need an International Driving Permit.

Where do I pick up and return the motorhome?

In Mijas Costa (Málaga), about 25 minutes from Málaga airport. There is free parking for your car while you travel. We can also deliver it to your home, hotel or the airport within 40 km (Fuengirola, Benalmádena, Torremolinos, Málaga, Marbella…) for an extra charge that we tell you when you book.

How many people can travel and sleep in it?

It is approved for 5 people. It has two twin beds at the back and a drop-down bed above the lounge.

How much is the deposit?

€900, left on a credit card when you collect the motorhome and returned after we have checked it on your return. If any damage needs assessing, the maximum period is 30 days. To confirm the booking you pay 30 % of the rental, and the rest on pick-up day.

Are pets allowed?

No. Sorry, pets are not allowed in the motorhome.

Can I travel to Portugal or other countries?

Yes. You can go to Portugal and much of Europe (France, Italy, Germany, the United Kingdom and others). Let us know a week in advance so we can extend the insurance. We include a road map of Spain and Portugal.

I've never travelled in a motorhome. Is it difficult?

No. Before you leave we calmly explain how everything works (water, gas, electricity, emptying the tanks) and give you a travel manual. During the rental you have 24-hour roadside assistance and our phone number.

What if I have to cancel?

If you cancel 30 days or more in advance, 50 % of the rental is charged; between 29 and 16 days, 80 %; with 15 days or less, 100 % of the booking is lost.

What if the motorhome isn't free on my dates?

Message us anyway. We work with other small motorhome rental businesses in the area and, if we're not available, we'll put you in touch with one we trust.

Which motorhome routes can you do from Málaga?

Our customers' favourites are the Costa de la Luz in Cádiz, Huelva and Doñana, the Portuguese Algarve and Cabo de Gata in Almería. Also the white villages and Ronda, Granada with the Alpujarra, the Sierra de Cazorla, Córdoba and Seville, the Murcia coast, Extremadura, the Alentejo and Lisbon, or short 3-day trips to the Sierra de las Nieves and Antequera. You will find the day-by-day itineraries, with maps and campsites, on our routes page.
